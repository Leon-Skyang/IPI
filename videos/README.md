# IPI Process Video

[Open the MP4](IPI_Process_English_1080p.mp4) · [Direct download](https://github.com/Leon-Skyang/IPI/raw/refs/heads/main/videos/IPI_Process_English_1080p.mp4)

This English explainer follows the IPI workflow from preparation and PES membrane exchange to separate-channel sampling and immediate ferrozine measurement. It was made with the existing IPI Three.js model and HyperFrames. The narration is AI-generated British English; background music is lowered during speech.

- Duration: 90 seconds.
- Resolution and frame rate: 1920 × 1080, 30 fps.
- Format: MP4, H.264 video and AAC audio.
- File size: 25,497,405 bytes.
- SHA-256: `cc55a5371358b01098094bfc507d03b56e94eae17fbff805b775dd8460127c17`.

Particle sizes, transport speed, colour development and compressed experimental time are explanatory illustrations, not measured results. PES is the exchange surface; PTFE provides the dense, nonporous liquid-flow connection.
